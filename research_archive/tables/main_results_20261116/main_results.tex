\subsection{Main Results}

\paragraph{Homogeneous Distillation on CIFAR-100.}
Table~\ref{tab:cifar_homo} organizes the homogeneous benchmark into
six teacher--student pairs from the ResNet, WideResNet, and VGG families.
Among the currently reconciled local results, ACD obtains $78.36\%$
Top-1 accuracy on ResNet32$\times$4 $\to$ ResNet8$\times$4 and
$75.48\%$ on VGG13 $\to$ VGG8. The former is a three-seed archived
mean, while the latter averages two paired runs.
The recorded ResNet comparison reports $77.40\%$ for DKD.
Other benchmark cells remain unfilled pending result recovery and
protocol verification; the reference teacher/student accuracies
are not substituted for local checkpoint evaluations.

\paragraph{Heterogeneous Distillation on CIFAR-100.}
Table~\ref{tab:cifar_hetero} organizes five cross-family pairs,
covering ResNet/WRN teachers with ShuffleNet students and VGG/ResNet
teachers with MobileNetV1 students.
The available ACD results are $78.86\%$ on WRN40-2 $\to$
ShuffleNetV1 and $69.71\%$ on VGG13 $\to$ MobileNetV1,
each based on two archived runs.
For the other three pairs, newly supplied historical screenshot
transcriptions provide relation-only and ETF-initialized readings,
shown separately in Table~\ref{tab:etf_user_reported}.
Their evaluation protocol and equivalence to the ACD configuration
remain to be verified, so they are not merged with the archived means.
Results obtained by combining ACD with
feature-distillation pipelines are reported separately in
Table~\ref{tab:dropin}.

\paragraph{Controlled Comparisons.}
The two VGG comparisons in Table~\ref{tab:cifar_paired} provide
paired evidence within the broader benchmark layout.
ACD exceeds affine-head KD by $1.09$ percentage points on
VGG13 $\to$ VGG8 and $1.93$ percentage points on
VGG13 $\to$ MobileNetV1; both seeds show positive gains.
On the latter pair, ACD also exceeds angular-head KL by $3.12$
percentage points and full standardized KL by $0.74$ percentage points.
In contrast, full standardized KL remains slightly better on
VGG13 $\to$ VGG8 ($75.55\%$ versus $75.48\%$).
These controlled findings indicate pair-dependent benefits and do not
establish superiority across all eleven benchmark pairs.

\paragraph{Historical ETF-Initialization Comparison.}
Table~\ref{tab:etf_user_reported} preserves five user-reported comparisons
between relation-only supervision and its ETF-initialized variant.
The reported differences are $-0.50$, $+0.24$, $-0.10$, $+0.84$, and
$+0.30$ percentage points, giving an unweighted mean of $+0.16$ points.
Three pairs improve and two decline, suggesting pair-dependent benefits
rather than a uniform gain. The evaluation split, checkpoint selection,
number of runs, and matching of training configurations remain unverified;
these records are therefore kept separate from the reconciled ACD means
and do not establish statistical significance or the proposed geometric mechanism.

\paragraph{Results on ImageNet-1K.}
Table~\ref{tab:imagenet} records the currently available baseline results
for ResNet50 $\to$ MobileNetV2. The pretrained teacher obtains
$76.146\%$ Top-1 and $92.872\%$ Top-5 accuracy on the complete
50,000-image validation set. The CE-trained student reaches
$68.662\%$ Top-1 and $88.798\%$ Top-5 accuracy after 100 epochs.
Final ordinary-KD and verified full-run ACD results are not yet available
in this snapshot. These baselines establish the large-scale evaluation
setting; an ACD scalability conclusion requires the completed matched
distillation experiments.

\subsection{ACD in Existing Distillation Pipelines}

We examine compatibility with feature-based distillation by replacing
the output-level KL term in AT and ReviewKD-style pipelines while
keeping their feature objectives and adapters fixed.
Table~\ref{tab:dropin} separates the effect of replacing the loss on an
existing angular head from the joint change of the classifier and loss.
On ResNet32$\times$4 $\to$ ShuffleNetV1, replacing angular-head KL
with SICD improves the AT pipeline from $77.60\%$ to $78.97\%$,
a gain of $1.37$ percentage points; the gain over the affine-head
AT+KL baseline is $1.67$ percentage points.
For ReviewKD-style distillation on WRN40-2 $\to$ WRN40-1,
the corresponding loss-only gain is $0.06$ percentage points
($75.54\%$ to $75.60\%$), while the final result remains
$0.14$ percentage points below the affine-head baseline.
These single-seed pilots demonstrate that conditional output supervision
can be incorporated into existing feature-distillation pipelines,
with benefits that vary across settings.

Replacing KL with SICD on the same angular classifier preserves the
student inference model. However, the implemented gradient-norm
matching requires additional training computation: the archived
controlled benchmark records $30.04$ ms per update for matched
conditional distillation versus $15.09$ ms for angular-head KL.
Accordingly, these experiments support compatibility and selected
accuracy gains, rather than universal improvements or unchanged
training cost.
