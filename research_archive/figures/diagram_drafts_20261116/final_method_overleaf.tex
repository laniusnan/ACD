  \caption{\textbf{Overview of ACI and SICD.}
  ACI parameterizes student logits using feature--classifier
  cosine similarities with a shared scale, removing explicit
  feature magnitude, class-specific weight norms, and biases.
  The teacher retains its original classifier.
  SICD standardizes teacher and student logits and matches
  their non-target conditional distributions, weighted by
  the teacher's non-target probability mass.
  A detached gradient-strength matching factor calibrates
  the conditional objective against standardized full KL.
  The student is trained jointly with ground-truth
  cross-entropy supervision.}
  \label{fig:method_overview}
