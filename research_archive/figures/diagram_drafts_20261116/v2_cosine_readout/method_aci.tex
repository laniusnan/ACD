\paragraph{Angular Coordinate Interface (ACI).}
The preceding analysis motivates separating directional category relations from
the magnitudes and offsets introduced by a classifier's parameterization.
Let $\mathbf{h}_s(x)\in\mathbb{R}^{d_s}$ denote the student feature and
$\mathbf{w}_{s,k}$ the classifier weight for class $k$. A conventional affine
readout mixes these quantities:
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
    z^{\mathrm{lin}}_{s,k}
    &= \mathbf{w}_{s,k}^{\top}\mathbf{h}_s+b_{s,k}\\
    &= \|\mathbf{h}_s\|_2\|\mathbf{w}_{s,k}\|_2
      \cos\theta_{s,k}+b_{s,k}.
  \end{aligned}
  \label{eq:aci_affine}
\end{equation}
ACI replaces this student readout with a bias-free cosine classifier.
We normalize each sample feature and each class-weight vector independently:
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
    \hat{\mathbf{h}}_s&=\frac{\mathbf{h}_s}{\|\mathbf{h}_s\|_2},\qquad
    \hat{\mathbf{w}}_{s,k}=\frac{\mathbf{w}_{s,k}}{\|\mathbf{w}_{s,k}\|_2},\\
    z_{s,k}&=s\,\hat{\mathbf{w}}_{s,k}^{\top}\hat{\mathbf{h}}_s
            =s\cos\theta_{s,k},
  \end{aligned}
  \label{eq:aci_cosine}
\end{equation}
where $s>0$ is shared across samples and classes and is fixed to $30$ in our
implementation; numerical denominator protection is omitted for clarity.
For nonzero vectors outside the denominator-protection regime, positive
rescaling of a feature or an individual class weight leaves this readout
unchanged. Thus, feature magnitudes and class-specific weight norms no longer
directly modulate the forward scores, while the feature and class directions
remain learnable. This is a classifier parameterization, not an additional
cosine-attraction loss: neither a fixed class-anchor arrangement nor an ETF
constraint is imposed.

ACI modifies only the student head; the frozen teacher retains its original
classifier. The two models communicate through their class-indexed output
vectors, without requiring equal feature dimensions or direct feature alignment.
The student scores feed the label cross-entropy objective and the subsequent
SICD branch, which standardizes outputs and selects conditional non-target
supervision. ACI therefore specifies the student readout, whereas SICD specifies
the distillation signal. We do not assume that normalization eliminates teacher
parameterization effects, keeps raw feature norms constant, or guarantees
compatible CE and KD gradients.
